Legity League privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026. Legity League is a small sleep league for friends, run by an individual (Liam Highducheck).

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, name and the display name you choose.
  • Sleep data: the sleep sessions and sleep stages (awake, light/core, deep, REM) that your phone's health store holds, read from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android, or typed in by you. Nothing else is read from the health store (no heart rate, steps, location or other health data), and the app never writes to it.
  • League data: your groups, scores, rankings and settings such as your bedtime goal and time zone.

How it is used

Only to run the league: to score your nights, show your results to you and to the members of the groups you join, and to post league results (for example to a group's Discord channel, if the group sets that up). Sleep data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone outside your groups and the services below.

Who can see it

Members of a group you join can see your nightly sleep totals, scores and records in that group. Nobody else can see your data. You can leave a group at any time.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase database (Supabase Inc., hosted on AWS). The app and its web version are delivered through Expo (EAS) and GitHub Pages. Data is encrypted in transit.

Deleting your data

In the app go to Me → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your sleep data from our database. Data that stays in Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone is not affected, and you can withdraw the app's access to it at any time in the Health or Health Connect settings.
